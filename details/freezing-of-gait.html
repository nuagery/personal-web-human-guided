Freezing of Gait Prediction and Monitoring

A literature review of wearable sensing approaches for Parkinson’s disease.

Literature reviewSensorsParkinson’s

Research question

How can wearable sensing support monitoring and prediction of freezing of gait in Parkinson’s disease?

Approach

The review compares more than 30 papers, considering sensor placement, comfort and accuracy across wearable approaches.

Publication

Freezing of Gait Prediction and Monitoring in the Treatment of Parkinson’s Disease. DOI: 10.54254/2755-2721/2026.TJ30921. This is a literature review, not a clinical trial.
